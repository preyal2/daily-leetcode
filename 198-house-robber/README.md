<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/house-robber">House Robber</a></h2> <img src='https://img.shields.io/badge/Difficulty-Medium-orange' alt='Difficulty: Medium' /><hr><p>You are a professional robber planning to rob houses along a street. Each house has a certain amount of money stashed. Adjacent houses have security systems connected that will automatically contact the police if two adjacent houses were broken into on the same night.</p>
<p>Given an integer array <code>nums</code> representing the amount of money of each house, return <em>the maximum amount of money you can rob tonight <strong>without alerting the police</strong></em>.</p>
